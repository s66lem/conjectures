# Internal publication audit (2026-11-24)

**Disposition:** pass for both the unconditional proof of Conjecture 5.6 and the disproof of literal Hypothesis 3.2(1)–(2) against the exact definitions of `arXiv:2610.09650`; publication as an unrefereed, non-Lean-verified result is appropriate. No claim of external acceptance or exclusive priority.

- Source: Definitions 2.1, 3.1, 5.1, Hypothesis 3.2, Lemma 4.7, Theorem 5.2, and Conjecture 5.6 were read verbatim from [arXiv:2610.09650](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.09650). "Stacked" means support size 1 (any positive pebble count on that vertex), "cleared" means total size 1, "almost stacked" means at most one vertex carries more than 1 pebble, and Hypothesis 3.2 explicitly specifies the *least* integer t ≥ 2.
- Claim 1 (literal Hypothesis 3.2): ordinary stackability and clearability are monotone above |V(G)| (Lemma 4.7), whereas almost-stacked stackability and clearability are non-monotone. Independent BFS verifiers confirm: on P₄, sizes 2..11 each admit a bad almost stacked configuration, all 32 almost stacked configurations of size 12 stack, `(9,0,0,3)` of size 12 does not stack, bad almost stacked configurations reappear at sizes 13 and 14 (`(13,0,0,1)`), and `stack(P₄) = 15`. On G₅ (triangle 0–1–2 with tail 2–3–4), sizes 2..15 each admit a bad almost stacked configuration, all 80 almost stacked configurations of size 16 clear, `(0,0,0,2,14)` does not clear, bad almost stacked configurations reappear at sizes 17 and 21 (`(0,0,0,0,21)`), and `clear(G₅) = 22`. Across all 12,112 connected graphs with n ≤ 8, literal ASH fails on 2,580 graph-parameter pairs while the monotone repair `1 + max{t : bad AS config of size t}` holds on all 23,971 checked pairs.
- Claim 2 (Conjecture 5.6): Theorem 5.2 of `arXiv:2610.09650` (lower bound `stack(T) ≥ estim(T)`) was re-checked and confirmed valid. Every lemma of the upper-bound proof (Lemmas A–F and the root-maximizer step) was independently audited: base cases n = 1, 2; disjointness and non-interference of sibling subtrees in `Run(y)` together with the pebble-balance identity `P - 2D - o = h(y)` and strict legality thresholds (`≥ 3` before payments, `≥ 2` before rounds); monotonicity and majorization `τ_f ≤ τ`; liveness of ancestors `[y, w)` in Lemma D; injective termwise charging in Lemma E across all cases (off-path non-live, live internal, live leaf; path `k = 0`; `0 ≤ j ≤ k - 2`; and the combined `(p_{k-1}, w)` bound when `w` is a leaf, including `k = 1` where `p_{k-1} = r`); and `τ(a) < a` for all `a ∈ ℤ` ruling out an empty leaf maximizer.
- Computational verification: replayed `verify.py` (Python 3 standard library): both ASH BFS disproofs and 1,440 tree tests (n = 2..30, paths/stars/random/extremal/mod-3) passed with zero warnings. Separate exhaustive runs confirmed `stack(T) = estim(T)` on all 94 trees n ≤ 9 and Lemma E on 1.51 × 10⁹ cases across all trees n ≤ 11.
