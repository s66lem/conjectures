# Negative Answer to Basak–Bijender–Kumar Question 4.14 (Waldschmidt Constant of Monomial Ideals, `arXiv:2611.19424v1`)

[![DOI](https://zenodo.org/badge/DOI/10.5281/zenodo.24558264.svg)](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24558264)

**Status:** Disproved with an explicit counterexample ($\alpha(I) = 4$, big-height $e = 14$, $\hat{\alpha}(I) = 26/23 < 17/15$) and an infinite two-parameter family — **Lean 4 verified** (zero `sorry`, zero custom axioms, zero `native_decide`).

*Lean scope note:* `Conjectures.BasakWaldschmidtMonomial` formally proves over `MvPolynomial` that $q_b^m$ is $P_b$-primary, $I = \bigcap_{b \in B} q_b$ is irredundant with $\mathrm{Min}(I) = \{P_b\}$ (each $P_b$ generated by $r + 1$ variables), $I^m R_{P_b} \cap R = q_b^m$, $\alpha(I) = T$, $\hat{\alpha}(I) = 2r/(2r - T + 1)$, and the strict bound violation for $e := r + 1$. The standard identifications $\mathrm{Ass}(I) = \mathrm{Min}(I)$ and $\mathrm{ht}(P_b) = r + 1$ are proved in `PROOF.md` and `paper.tex`.

## Target Question

N. Basak, Bijender, and A. Kumar, *On the Waldschmidt constant of monomial ideals*, [arXiv:2611.19424v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.19424v1) (14 Nov 2026), Question 4.14:

> **Question 4.14.** Let $I \subset R$ be a monomial ideal of big-height $e$. Is the lower bound
> $$\hat{\alpha}(I) \ge \frac{\alpha(I) + e - 1}{e + 1}$$
> always satisfied?

Here $R = K[x_1, \dots, x_N]$, $\alpha(J)$ is the least degree of a nonzero homogeneous element in $J$, $I^{(m)}$ is the $m$-th symbolic power of $I$, $\hat{\alpha}(I) = \lim_{m \to \infty} \alpha(I^{(m)}) / m = \inf_{m \ge 1} \alpha(I^{(m)}) / m$ is the Waldschmidt constant, and $e = \max\{\mathrm{ht}(P) : P \in \mathrm{Ass}(I)\}$ is the big-height of $I$.

## Main Result

**Theorem (Negative answer to Question 4.14).** For every pair of integers $T \ge 4$ and $r \ge 1$ satisfying $r(T - 3) > T(T - 1)$, let $B = \{b \in \mathbb{N}^r : b_1 + \dots + b_r = T - 1\}$, work in $R = K[x_1, \dots, x_r, y_b : b \in B]$, and define
$$q_b = \bigl(x_1^{b_1 + 1}, \dots, x_r^{b_r + 1},\, y_b\bigr), \qquad I = \bigcap_{b \in B} q_b.$$
Then $I$ is an unmixed monomial ideal with $\mathrm{Ass}(I) = \mathrm{Min}(I) = \{P_b : b \in B\}$, where $P_b = (x_1, \dots, x_r, y_b)$, so all associated primes are minimal of height $e = r + 1$, $I^{(m)} = \bigcap_{b \in B} q_b^m$ under every standard definition of symbolic powers, and
$$\alpha(I) = T, \qquad \hat{\alpha}(I) = \frac{2r}{2r - T + 1} < \frac{T + r}{r + 2} = \frac{\alpha(I) + e - 1}{e + 1}.$$

In the headline instance $(T, r) = (4, 13)$ ($|B| = \binom{15}{3} = 455$ components, $468$ variables):
$$\alpha(I) = 4, \qquad e = 14, \qquad \hat{\alpha}(I) = \frac{26}{23} = \frac{390}{345} < \frac{391}{345} = \frac{17}{15} = \frac{\alpha(I) + e - 1}{e + 1}.$$

Additional family instances include $(T, r) = (4, 14), (5, 11), (6, 11), (7, 11)$.

## Proof Summary

1. **Primary decomposition and symbolic powers:** Each $q_b$ is generated by pure powers of the $r + 1$ variables of $P_b = (x_1, \dots, x_r, y_b)$, so every power $q_b^m$ is $P_b$-primary. The primes $P_b$ are pairwise incomparable of height $r + 1$, and $I = \bigcap_{b \in B} q_b$ is irredundant (witnessed by $\prod_{b' \ne b} y_{b'}$), giving $\mathrm{Ass}(I) = \mathrm{Min}(I) = \{P_b\}$, big-height $e = r + 1$, and $I^{(m)} = \bigcap_{b \in B} q_b^m$.
2. **Initial degree $\alpha(I) = T$:** Clearly $x_1^T \in q_b$ for all $b \in B$ (since $b_1 + 1 \le T$). For any monomial $x^c y^d \in I$ with $s = |c| \le T - 1$, every $b \in B$ satisfying $b \ge c$ componentwise misses all $x_j^{b_j+1}$, forcing $d_b \ge 1$; since $|\{b \in B : b \ge c\}| = \binom{r + T - 2 - s}{T - 1 - s} \ge T - s$, we have $|c| + |d| \ge T$.
3. **Exact Waldschmidt constant $\hat{\alpha}(I) = \frac{2r}{2r - T + 1}$:**
   - *Upper bound:* For every $k \ge 1$ and $b \in B$, writing $p, q, \beta$ for the counts of entries equal to $0$, equal to $1$, and $\ge 2$ in $b$, we have $\sum_{j=1}^r \lfloor 2k/(b_j + 1) \rfloor \ge k(2p + q) = k(2r - q - 2\beta) \ge (2r - T + 1)k$. Thus $\prod_{j=1}^r x_j^{2k} \in I^{((2r - T + 1)k)}$, giving $\alpha(I^{((2r - T + 1)k)}) \le 2rk$ and $\hat{\alpha}(I) \le \frac{2r}{2r - T + 1}$.
   - *Lower bound:* Averaging the fractional membership inequality $\sum_j c_j / (b_j + 1) + d_b \ge m$ over the $\binom{r}{T - 1}$ vectors $b \in B$ with $T - 1$ ones and $r - T + 1$ zeros yields $\alpha(I^{(m)}) / m \ge \frac{2r}{2r - T + 1}$ for every $m \ge 1$.
4. **Violation:** $\frac{2r}{2r - T + 1} < \frac{T + r}{r + 2} \iff r(T - 3) > T(T - 1)$.
