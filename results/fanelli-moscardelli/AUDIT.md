# Internal publication audit (2026-11-24)

**Disposition:** pass for the written disproof of the exact published and formalized statement; publication as an unrefereed, non-Lean-verified result is appropriate. No claim of external acceptance or exclusive priority.

- Source: read directly from [Fanelli–Moscardelli (ECAI 2025)](https://doi.org/10.3233/FAIA251234) and `google-deepmind/formal-conjectures` (`FormalConjectures/Paper/ApproximatelyStableMatching.lean` and `FormalConjecturesForMathlib/Combinatorics/Roommates.lean`). One-based `partnerRank` assigns single agents rank N; a pair {i, j} k-blocks a matching μ when `rank_i(j) + k ≤ rank_i(μ(i))` and `rank_j(i) + k ≤ rank_j(μ(j))`. The theorem `stability_threshold_tight` asserts `∃ N₀ ≥ 3, ∀ N ≥ N₀, stabilityThreshold N = (N + 1) / 2`. Proving `K(2m + 1) ≤ m` for every m ≥ 3 refutes both the Lean statement and the paper’s conjecture at every odd N ≥ 7.
- Proof audit: every lemma (§§0–7) was independently audited line by line. Lemma 1 (near-perfect m-stability criterion) has no off-by-one error on the `+ m - 1` threshold. Section 1 handles `|W| = 1`, rules out 2-cycles on W to force `r_u = 1` and injectivity of `x`, and verifies the exchange step across both the no-added-edge and added-edge `zu` cases. By (R1), `|J| ≥ m - 1 > 0`. In (R2), Sumner’s characterization of connected randomly matchable graphs as K_{2r} or K_{t,t} (*J. Graph Theory* 3 (1979), 183–186) was additionally cross-checked by brute force on all labelled graphs on 2, 4, and 6 vertices. Lemmas B, RB, Property (*), GA, K2J, Corollary S, MJ, and D were verified across all edge cases (including `|I| = 3`, `x_s = e` impossibility, `x(x_s) ≠ w`, and the `u → y` chain contradiction in K2J). The component-wise counting bounds for K₂, K_{2r}, and K_{t,t} (where the quadratic terms cancel identically to `t(2m - n) ≥ 2t`) combine with `L ≤ |D| ≤ b - κ` to force `a = 0`, `d ≡ 1`, `|I| = 3`, `L = 0`, `κ = b`, and a tournament on non-partners with `|out(z)| = m - 2`. Section 7 closes the all-K₂ case in three lines using `m ≥ 3` (the sole step requiring `m ≥ 3`, matching `K(5) = 3`).
- Computational verification: an independent from-scratch audit implementation of §§1–7 passed ~45,000 uniform, residual, K_{t,t}, Σ*, and all-K₂ profiles for N = 7–13 with zero assertion failures (and at N = 5 failed only at §7, never earlier). Replaying the published standalone script `verify.py` passed all 1,800 profiles across N ∈ {7, 9, 11, 13, 15, 21} with zero warnings. Separate DRAT-verified SAT runs (`drat-trim` `s VERIFIED`) independently confirmed exact values K(7) = 3, K(8) = 3, K(9) = 4, and K(11) = 5.
